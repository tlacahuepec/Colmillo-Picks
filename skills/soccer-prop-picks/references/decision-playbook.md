# Decision Playbook

Use this checklist before finalizing any soccer prop recommendation.

## Required Reasoning Checkpoints

1. **Lineups, injuries, suspensions**
   - Verify lineup status for both teams (confirmed vs projected).
   - Account for notable injuries/suspensions that alter role or minutes.
   - If lineup certainty is weak, disclose the assumption and reduce conviction.

2. **Form + standings + home/away**
   - Cross-check recent form and standings motivation context.
   - Include venue context (home/away) when explaining role opportunity and game script.
   - Flag contradictions where form suggests one script and standings urgency suggests another.

3. **Weather impact**
   - Validate weather summary with wind/precipitation context.
   - Explicitly reflect adverse weather penalties in rationale when flagged by the scorer.
   - Avoid weather-driven narratives unless weather signals are present in the data.

4. **Market agreement sanity**
   - Confirm that cross-book snapshots support a coherent market view.
   - When disagreement is high or sources are sparse, lower confidence or issue no-bet.
   - Keep market sanity conclusions tied to scorer-produced flags rather than manual odds math.
